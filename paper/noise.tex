\section{Whole-cell perturbations}\label{sec:noise}
All perturbations in this section are arbitrary Hermitian operators on
the legal whole-cell space, bounded in total norm. A rank-one operator
on a history is generally nonlocal in the physical lattice. No random
or generic noise ensemble is being assumed.

First, there can be no universal saturation of entanglement for every
norm-$\eta$ perturbation. For example $V=\eta H/\norm H$ preserves
the ground space and all its entropies. A positive perturbation
annihilating that space gives another example. The energy comparison
of Corollary~\ref{cor:perturbed} remains a lower certificate for every
perturbation, but does not by itself supply an upper bound.

\begin{proposition}[A collapse perturbation]\label{prop:collapse}
Suppose an honest uniform history $\Phi_0$ of a product system input
has loss $\ell_0\leq1$, so its entropy is at most $\log_2|X|$ and
its energy is $\ell_0/M$. For $V=-\eta\ket{\Phi_0}\bra{\Phi_0}$,
every unit ground vector $\Psi$ obeys
\begin{equation}\label{eq:collapse}
 |\langle\Phi_0,\Psi\rangle|^2\geq1-\frac{\ell_0}{M\eta}.
\end{equation}
If $L_A=\log_2D_A$ is an actual local support logarithm and
$M\eta/L_A^2\to\infty$, then
$S_A(\Psi)\leq\log_2|X|+o(1)$.
\end{proposition}
\begin{proof}
Positivity of $H$ and the test vector give
\[
 -\eta|\langle\Phi_0,\Psi\rangle|^2
 \leq E_0(H+V)\leq\ell_0/M-\eta.
\]
Rearrange to prove \eqref{eq:collapse}. The pure-state trace distance
is at most $\sqrt{\ell_0/(M\eta)}$. Apply entropy continuity with
the specified support dimension. Its error is bounded by that distance
times $L_A$, plus binary entropy, and hence tends to zero.
\end{proof}

The condition involves dimension as well as $M$. In the conditional
layout, $L_A=\operatorname{poly}(N)$ and $M$ grows exponentially
in $N$, so fixed $\eta>0$ suffices. Mere divergence of $M$ without
dimension control would not suffice.

We next specify a different cell: a free-depth menu. For each
admissible even depth $N_0\leq j\leq N$, it contains a classical
sector running the admitted dimension-matched strategy at depth $j$,
all with the same padded clock volume $M$. Unused system registers
are frozen locally, and consistent depth labels are locally available
to both players. The extraction uses a product rewinding on the whole
direct sum and a uniform coefficient $C'_N$ polynomial in $N$.
The menu has actual local support logarithm $L_A=\operatorname{poly}(N)$.
Assume the uniform reference estimates and the depth-wise dimension
law $\log_2d_j=4\alpha j+o(j)$; honest histories have entropy at most
$4\alpha j+o(j)+O(1)$ and target loss
$\eps_j=C_1 16^{-j}$. The same fixed game has both prices with
coefficient $\alpha>0$. Implementing this menu in a local material
is an extra layout obligation.

Define $S_{\rm worst}(N,\eta)$ as the infimum of $S_A(\Psi)$ over
all allowed $V$ with $\norm V\leq\eta$ and all unit pure ground
vectors $\Psi$ of $H+V$.

\begin{theorem}[Worst-case free-depth law]\label{thm:noise}
In this menu model, put $a=M\eta$ and
$r_N=\log_2(1/\max(\eps_N,a))$. In joint limits with
$r_N/\log(N+2)\to\infty$,
\begin{equation}\label{eq:noise}
 S_{\rm worst}(N,\eta)=\alpha r_N(1+o(1)).
\end{equation}
For every perturbation the lower bound holds. The saturation upper
bound uses a chosen rank-one whole-cell perturbation. In the separate
collapse regime $M\eta\gg L_A^2$, Proposition~\ref{prop:collapse}
gives the bounded upper entropy.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
The deepest admitted history has energy at most $\eps_N/M$.
For every perturbed ground vector, the energy comparison and uniform
extraction yield
\[
 S_A\geq\price\bigl(C'_N[\eps_N+2M\eta]\bigr).
\]
In the stated regime the effective loss tends to zero and its
negative logarithm is $r_N+O(\log N)$. The imported price therefore
gives $S_A\geq\alpha r_N(1-o(1))$.

We first justify the unperturbed upper bound for the menu. The system
dimension argument of Proposition~\ref{prop:frustration} in sector
$j$ gives
\[
 e_j\geq\frac{16^{-j+o(j)}}{\operatorname{poly}(N)M}.
\]
Any fixed bounded sector also has positive fixed dimension-constrained
loss, because the game's divergent price forbids a perfect strategy
in that dimension. For each fixed $\delta>0$, sectors
$j\leq(1-\delta)N$ are eventually above
$\eps_N/M$. Thus every unperturbed minimizing sector has
$j=N-o(N)$. Sector-wise normalized reference estimates squeeze its
entropy to $4\alpha N+o(N)$. Superpositions of at most $O(N)$
locally distinguished sectors contribute at most $\log_2O(N)$
additional entropy. Uniform continuity errors still vanish on the
assumed support, proving the unperturbed upper bound. In particular
one can always choose a pure unperturbed ground vector with this bound.

If $a\leq\eps_N$, choose $V=0$ for the desired upper bound.
If $a>\eps_N$, fix $p>2$ and choose the \emph{smallest} admissible
even depth $j$ satisfying
\begin{equation}\label{eq:depthchoice}
 \eps_j\leq aL_A^{-p}.
\end{equation}
When such a depth exists, the logarithmic regime ensures that it
eventually exceeds the fixed lower cutoff and
\[
 4j=\log_2(1/a)+p\log_2L_A+O(1).
\]
Take its honest history $\Phi_j$ and
$V=-\eta\ket{\Phi_j}\bra{\Phi_j}$. The same variational argument
as \eqref{eq:collapse} gives overlap at least $1-L_A^{-p}$ with
every perturbed ground vector. Entropy continuity costs
$O(L_A^{1-p/2})+h_2(L_A^{-p/2})=o(1)$. Therefore
\[
 S_A\leq4\alpha j+o(j)+O(1)
       =\alpha\log_2(1/a)(1+o(1)),
\]
since $\log L_A=O(\log N)=o(r_N)$.
If \eqref{eq:depthchoice} would require $j>N$, then
$1<a/\eps_N<L_A^p$ up to a fixed endpoint factor. In that case
$V=0$ again gives the upper bound, as the difference in the relevant
negative logarithms is only $O(\log N)=o(r_N)$. This handles the
precision floor and completes the saturation proof.
\end{proof}

The effective variable is $M\eta$, together with the extraction
coefficient. In a noise-dominated logarithmic regime, changing $T$ at
fixed total $\eta$ and fixed $k$ inserts the term
$-k\alpha\log_2(T+1)$. This observation is confined to that regime
and this menu model. Open-clock perturbation analyses such as
\cite{tempel} concern their specified dynamics and do not establish
a local-noise version of \eqref{eq:noise}.

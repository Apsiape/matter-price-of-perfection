\section{Scope and further questions}\label{sec:scope}
The explicit gapped construction programs a logarithmic entropy
coefficient of a growing sub-cell region, and a power exponent of its
Schmidt rank. It does not classify every entanglement observable in
every gapped spin system. The arithmetic ceiling requires effective
algebraic cell data, a specified region, and a specified state rule.
Our correlation ceiling requires norm-quasi-local measurement effects.

The referee theorem concerns an exact legal-space operator with local
rewinding. A physical implementation must prove its legality,
initialization, transport and interface properties. For positive cell
energies it must also prove the local charging hypothesis. Standard
history constructions make these requirements meaningful but do not
automatically discharge them. The conditional theorem records exactly
what the game price would then force, without asserting that this
particular material has been constructed.

The perturbation theorem concerns a specified free-depth cell and
arbitrary total-norm operators. The resource bounds are necessary
inequalities. The thermal pair calculation retains mutual information;
it supplies no all-temperature entanglement claim. General robust
exponents, generic local-noise saturation, and matching physical
preparation costs are not conclusions of this paper. Further questions
include quantitative protection conditions for specified microscopic
designs and sharper extraction constants under additional circuit or
ground-state assumptions. No conjectural theorem is used in any proof.

\section{How this work was done}\label{sec:methods}
The authors developed the mathematical argument with assistance from
AI models from more than one developer. They take responsibility for
the claims, the scope of the imports, and the distinction between a
proof, a conditional implication, and a finite numerical check.
The manuscript uses the released ruler and game theorems at the
numbered locations stated in Section~\ref{sec:imports}. Its new
arguments are written above; those imported proofs are not reproduced.

The ancillary directory contains a sequential runner, source files,
recorded outputs, a summary with measured peak memory, and a SHA-256
manifest. Four supplied checks are retained for the elementary
two-clock inequality, perfect-stripe arithmetic and pair samples,
two-clock resistance extraction, and three-clock extraction. Their
numerical calculations use floating-point eigenvalues. A description
in the pair-sampling script is restricted to the finite scope actually
checked. Independent checks test rational staircase identities,
finite-grid pseudoinverses, the extraction operator inequality for
complex projection data, normalized reference estimates, mirror-field
formulas, the depth-selection rule, and thermal-pair formulas.

Scripts run sequentially with numerical-library thread counts set to
one and a monitored combined process-memory ceiling below one gigabyte.
Passing these checks is finite consistency evidence. It does not
certify an uncomputable limiting real, a translation-invariant rule
table for the referee, the local positive-energy charging hypothesis,
or any statistical claim about local noise. The analytic proofs and
their stated hypotheses govern all infinite-volume conclusions.
